# Privacy Policy — New French

Effective date: June 23, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the iOS app **New French: учить язык** published by Azat Ismagilov.

## Data Collection

The current version of the app does not collect personal data.

The app does not require account registration and does not ask the user to submit personal information.

## Local App Data

The app may store learning progress, onboarding choices, settings, selected lesson state and practice results locally on the user's device. This data is used only to provide the learning experience inside the app.

Local app data is not transmitted to the developer's servers in the current version.

## Microphone And Speech Recognition

The app may request microphone and speech recognition permissions for pronunciation and dictation practice. Audio and recognition results are used only to provide the exercise experience requested by the user.

## Analytics And Advertising

The current version of the app does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs or tracking technologies.

## Children

The app is an educational language learning app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If the app adds analytics, ads, subscriptions, cloud sync, accounts, or any server-side data processing in a future version, this policy and the App Store privacy answers must be updated before release.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact: azatzidane@gmail.com
